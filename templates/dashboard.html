How to read these metrics

  • Accuracy: overall share of correct predictions.
  • Precision: among predicted completed bookings, how many were completed.
  • Recall: among completed bookings, how many the model found.
  • F1-score: balance between precision and recall.

For this dataset, the completed-booking class is the minority class. A high accuracy score can hide missed completed bookings.
